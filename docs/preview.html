Pinstripe

Current renders of every part, made offscreen from the real builds: the Qt widget style, the Plasma style, the GTK theme, the icons and the window decoration. Each section lists the measurements from Apple's June 2002 Aqua Human Interface Guidelines it follows.

Desktop

  • Menus and popups: pinstriped sheets (rows 255, 249, 238, 249), groups separated by space, selection as the striped blue gel.
  • Dock: milky glass flush with the bottom edge, black triangles under running apps.
  • Menu extras: solid glyphs from the Oxygen Pinstripe icons.

Widget style

  • Push buttons: 20 px high, 14 px end caps, 69 px for OK and Cancel; only the default button is blue and it pulses.
  • Check boxes and radio buttons: 18 × 18 including the shadow.
  • Pop-up menus: 20 px, text 9 px from the left edge, double-arrow cap.
  • Tabs: 23 px, 12 px text inset, centred over the 6-row accent bar (#0057ae, #a2d7f0, #a8d1ee, #51b0ea, #79ccff, #acefff) and its grey shadow; the selected tab morphs seamlessly into the blue strip without a dividing rim (Qt, Plasma and GTK). New
  • Text fields: 22 px with a 3 px inner shadow and the blue focus ring.
  • Scroll bars: 15 px white cylinder, thumb columns measured from the real thumb.

Dialogs and group boxes New

  • Group box: a fine etched line with square corners on the window stripes; the title sits on the top border in the small system font; nothing within 16 px of the border (HIG Figures 8-2 to 8-4).
  • Flat group: title followed by an etched separator 2 px to its right, as the HIG recommends instead of nested boxes.
  • Alert: 64 px application icon, message in the emphasized (bold) system font, informative text in the small system font, default button on the right (Figure 6-2).
  • Bevel buttons: curved glass cushions with a crisp top specular lens, cool silver mid-tone dip, upward bottom reflection and dark bottom rim; smoked glass when pressed or toggled on, labels in the 10 pt label font (Figures 7-14, 7-15). New

Lists, sliders and progress

  • Lists: rows at least 19 px (19 pt baseline to baseline), headings in the small system font. New
  • Sliders: 15 × 15 round thumb, 15 × 18 pointed when there are tick marks.
  • Progress: blue gel with moving bands; indeterminate is a barber pole; grey in inactive windows.

Menus

  • Rows: 19 px items, groups separated by 12 px of blank space, never lines (HIG chapter 4).
  • Text and shortcuts: text 23 px in; shortcuts as modifier symbols right-aligned against a key column, as on 10.2 (KDE's Ctrl is drawn as the Control symbol ⌃). New
  • Shadow: menus, help tags and pop-up lists ask KWin for a soft drop shadow, heavier below. New

Toolbars, docks, MDI windows and side tabs New

  • Toolbars: 32 × 32 icons above 11 pt small system font labels with no button frames, dotted separators and a 1 px #a3a3a3 bottom divider on the window pinstripes (HIG chapters 7 & 11). New

Window decoration New

  • Title bar: 22 px high, 13 px traffic-light gels 9 px in at a 22 px pitch whose glyphs appear on hover; window pinstripes darkening towards a #7f7f7f divider, black active title and #8b8b8b inactive title (Figures 5-10, 7-16). New

GTK 3 and GTK 4 New

  • Parity: 16 px progress fill rows, selected tabs morphing into the accent strip, glassy bevel buttons, flat disclosure triangles (white on selection), etched group boxes and small-font list headings and help tags. New

Oxygen Pinstripe icons New

  • 10.2 action icons: 111 master full-colour Aqua artworks (+200 freedesktop/GTK aliases) across 16, 22, 24, 32, 32@2x and 48 px, sampled from HIG Chapter 11 (Figures 11-18, 11-19): round clear-glass navigation discs (Back/Forward with triangle + twin dots, Help ?), sky-blue pinstriped folders with navy-outlined + badges, curled white pages, front-on desktop printer, Sherlock glass magnifier, yellow Compose pencil, perforated blue and crimson Mail stamps, Home cottage, ruby Favorites heart, crossed Utilities hammer & wrench and Disc Burner trefoil. New
  • Menu extras & symbolic glyphs: 426 names (139 distinct shapes) after the 10.2 menu extras, recolourable by KDE and GTK.
